ĀYĀMA आयाम

Metric elevation from a single image

Upload a nadir satellite or aerial image. ĀYĀMA estimates relative depth, harvests metric anchors from the scene — a public DEM where one is configured, water bodies, and cast-shadow geometry — solves a spatially varying calibration, and returns a metric surface you can inspect in 3D and download as GeoTIFF.

Read this before trusting a result This is a research proof of concept with a known, diagnosed failure: the calibration's scale field collapses to its floor and building relief is suppressed to a few centimetres. Expect good terrain and flattened structures. Every reconstruction states its own defects on the result screen. See section 4 of the README.

Options

A georeferenced GeoTIFF with sun angles reaches Tier A and produces the best result. A plain PNG or JPEG has no CRS and no sun metadata, so it drops to Tier C: trustworthy relative structure on an arbitrary datum. The tier actually used is reported with the result.
